Hi, I'm Julius. I work on making language models robust, aligned, and epistemically responsible. I'm a final year undergrad studying **Computer Science** and **Philosophy** at **Georgia Tech**, advised by Prof. Srijan Kumar. I'm also a research fellow at the [Cambridge-Boston Alignment Initiative](https://www.cbai.ai/), advised by Prof. Weiyan Shi.

I'm interested in the relationship between humans and AI systems, and how we make sure it goes well on both ends. My research interests are in (i) **epistemic impact**, studying how models shape what people know and believe, and further upstream, what they notice, find salient, and are able to think; (ii) **preference and value representation**, understanding and improving how models learn what people want; and (iii) **human-AI coevolution**, studying the loop between the two, where a model's picture of a person shapes what it shows them, and what it shows them reshapes the person.

Previously, I was a machine learning engineering intern at LinkedIn, and before that at Microsoft AI. Outside of research, I like playing the drums, looking for new [music](https://www.last.fm/user/juliusdcreator), grappling, and playing [chess](https://www.chess.com/member/juliusdcreator). Don't hesitate to get in touch — I'm always happy to hear from people!
